## 🔬 What I'm Working On

### 360° Multi-Camera Person Tracking & Re-ID · *Ongoing research*

I'm part of a team developing a person-tracking and re-identification pipeline for multi-camera environments.

- Comparing **OSNet, TransReID, and CLIP-ReID** under different image and evaluation conditions
- Working on **face/body feature fusion**, **global identity association**, and **HOTA ground-truth annotations**
- Investigating how to evaluate tracking and identity consistency more reliably

*This is ongoing collaborative research; the code and experimental results are not publicly released.*

### [Acute Appendicitis Analysis with U-Net](https://github.com/Wendylin0112/AOCR2024-AI-Challenge)

**MATLAB · Medical Imaging · 2D U-Net · Dice Loss**

I developed the **2D U-Net** pipeline for CT-image segmentation and scan-level classification, including preprocessing, augmentation, loss design, and post-processing.

- **Scan-level F1: 0.833** on a course-provided 20-patient evaluation set
- Explored mismatches between training metrics and predictions, along with data-splitting and evaluation limitations
- Team project recognized with an honorable mention at the CCU College of Science Poster Competition

[Code, report & poster →](https://github.com/Wendylin0112/AOCR2024-AI-Challenge)
