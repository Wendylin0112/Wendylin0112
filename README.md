### Acute Appendicitis Analysis with U-Net | 2025
*Medical Image Analysis course project · MATLAB · 2D U-Net · Dice loss*

- Took responsibility for the **2D U-Net** CT-image segmentation-to-classification pipeline, including preprocessing, augmentation, customized Dice loss, and post-processing.
- Achieved **scan-level F1 = 0.833** on a course-provided **20-patient evaluation set**. A teammate developed the nnU-Net comparison (reported F1 = 0.666); the two pipelines were not a controlled architecture-only comparison.
- Investigated mismatches between training metrics and actual predictions, and reflected on data-splitting and evaluation limitations.
- The team received an **Honorable Mention** in the CCU College of Science Poster Competition.
