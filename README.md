## 🔬 Research

**[360° Multi-Camera Person Re-ID & Tracking](./research/360-multicamera-reid.md)** · *Ongoing undergraduate research / industry collaboration*

- **Question:** How do panoramic distortion and viewpoint changes affect cross-camera identity association?
- **My work:** ReID model comparisons (OSNet / TransReID / CLIP-ReID), HOTA ground-truth preparation, and exploration of face/body fusion and one-to-one global-ID matching.
- **Status:** Investigating evaluation quality and association reliability. [Research overview](./research/360-multicamera-reid.md); working repository remains private.

**Acute Appendicitis Analysis via U-Net** · *Graduate-level medical imaging course project*

- **My work:** Developed the **2D U-Net** CT segmentation/classification branch with Dice-based loss and post-processing; a teammate independently developed **nnU-Net**.
- **Result:** **Scan-level F1 0.833** on a course-provided **20-patient** evaluation set (not a clinical benchmark). **CCU College of Science Poster Competition — Honorable Mention.**
- [My 2D U-Net](https://github.com/Wendylin0112/AOCR2024-AI-Challenge) · [Teammate's nnU-Net](https://github.com/zihyuhe/nnU-Net-med-img) · [Poster (PDF)](https://github.com/Wendylin0112/AOCR2024-AI-Challenge/blob/main/%E9%86%AB%E5%AD%B8%E5%BD%B1%E5%83%8Fproject%E5%A3%81%E5%A0%B1%E5%BD%B1%E5%8D%B0%E7%89%88%E6%9C%AC.pdf)
